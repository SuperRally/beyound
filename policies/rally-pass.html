Rally Pass — Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Introduction

Rally is committed to providing users with a simpler and more rewarding payment experience. As part of this commitment, Rally introduces Rally Pass, a prepaid platform-fee benefit that allows eligible users to pay a fixed fee upfront and transact on the Rally Platform without being separately charged applicable Platform Fees on eligible transactions, subject to the applicable Pass limits and these Terms and Conditions ("T&Cs").

Rally Pass is available only to users who are specifically identified as eligible by Rally.

By purchasing, activating, or using a Rally Pass, the user acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by these T&Cs.

Definitions

"Rally Pass" means a prepaid platform-fee benefit offered by Rally to eligible users under which the user pays a fixed upfront amount and, subject to the applicable Pass terms, is not separately charged Platform Fees on Eligible Transactions until the applicable Pass Limit is exhausted.

"Pass Fee" means the fixed amount payable by the user upfront for purchasing a Rally Pass.

"Pass Limit" means the maximum cumulative Transaction Value that may be processed under a particular Rally Pass before the Pass is considered exhausted.

"Eligible Transaction" means a transaction initiated and successfully completed by the user on the Rally Platform that qualifies for the benefits of the applicable Rally Pass, subject to the exclusions and limitations set out in these T&Cs.

"Transaction Value" means the total value of an Eligible Transaction, excluding Platform Fees, taxes, penalties, or other amounts separately payable by the user.

"Platform Fees" means the platform fees that Rally may otherwise charge on an Eligible Transaction in the absence of an applicable Rally Pass. Platform Fees do not include the underlying payment amount, statutory charges, taxes, or amounts payable to third parties unless expressly specified by Rally.

"Pass Exhaustion" means the point at which the cumulative Transaction Value of Eligible Transactions processed under a Rally Pass reaches the applicable Pass Limit.

"Pass Purchase Date" means the date on which the user successfully purchases a Rally Pass and the Pass Fee is successfully received by Rally.

Purchase and Activation

  • A Rally Pass may be purchased only by users who are identified as eligible by Rally.
  • The user shall be required to pay the applicable Pass Fee upfront through the payment methods made available on the Rally Platform.
  • A Rally Pass shall become active only after successful receipt and confirmation of the Pass Fee by Rally.
  • The user shall be able to view the applicable Pass details, including the Pass type, Pass Limit, applicable benefits, and Pass status on the Rally Platform, where such information is made available.
  • The Pass benefits are personal to the user who purchased the Pass and cannot be transferred, assigned, sold, or otherwise made available to another person or account.

Rally Pass Benefit

Upon successful activation of a Rally Pass, Rally shall not separately levy the applicable Platform Fees on Eligible Transactions during the period for which the Pass remains active and until the Pass Limit is exhausted.

The applicable Pass Limits are:

  • Turbo 10: Eligible Transactions of up to ₹10,00,000
  • Turbo 15: Eligible Transactions of up to ₹15,00,000
  • Turbo 25: Eligible Transactions of up to ₹25,00,000

For example, if a user purchases Turbo 10, the user may process cumulative Eligible Transactions of up to ₹10,00,000 under the Pass without being separately charged the applicable Platform Fees.

The Rally Pass benefit applies only to the Platform Fees expressly covered by the applicable Pass. Any tax, statutory charge, government levy, penalty, third-party charge, or other amount that is not expressly covered by the Rally Pass shall continue to be payable by the user. The Pass does not reduce, waive, or otherwise affect the underlying Transaction Value payable by the user.

Pass Utilisation

The Pass Limit shall be consumed based on the cumulative Transaction Value of Eligible Transactions processed under the Pass. The Pass Limit is calculated on the cumulative Transaction Value, and not on the amount of Platform Fees that the user would otherwise have paid.

For example, if a user purchases Turbo 10, which has a Pass Limit of ₹10,00,000, and subsequently makes Eligible Transactions of ₹2,00,000, ₹3,00,000, and ₹5,00,000, the cumulative Transaction Value shall be ₹10,00,000 and the Rally Pass shall be considered exhausted.

Transactions Exceeding the Remaining Pass Limit

Where the remaining Pass Limit is less than the value of a new Eligible Transaction, the Rally Pass benefit shall apply only to the portion of the transaction that falls within the remaining Pass Limit. The portion of the transaction that exceeds the remaining Pass Limit shall be subject to the applicable Platform Fees prevailing on the Rally Platform at the time of the transaction.

For example, if a user has a remaining Pass Limit of ₹20,000 and initiates an Eligible Transaction of ₹40,000:

  • The first ₹20,000 shall be covered under the Rally Pass and shall not attract the applicable Platform Fee; and
  • The remaining ₹20,000 shall be charged the applicable Platform Fee as per the prevailing rates.

Accordingly, the Rally Pass benefit shall apply only to the portion of the transaction within the remaining Pass Limit. Following such a transaction, the Pass shall be considered exhausted.

Transactions Covered

Unless otherwise specified for a particular Pass, Rally Pass benefits may apply to Eligible Transactions processed through the Rally Platform. Rally may specify certain transaction types, categories, payment methods, merchant categories, or use cases that are eligible or ineligible for Rally Pass benefits. The applicable eligibility criteria may be displayed to the user at the time of Pass purchase or within the Rally Platform.

Exclusions

Rally Pass benefits shall not apply to:

  • Utility bill payments, credit card bill payments, or other transaction categories specifically excluded by Rally;
  • Transactions that are failed, declined, cancelled, reversed, refunded, or otherwise not successfully completed;
  • Transactions involving fraudulent, suspicious, prohibited, or unlawful activity;
  • Transactions identified as self-funded, circular, artificial, or undertaken primarily to obtain or misuse Rally Pass benefits;
  • Transactions that violate Rally's Terms of Service or applicable laws;
  • Any statutory fee, government levy, tax, penalty, or third-party charge that is not expressly covered by the Rally Pass; and
  • Any other transaction category that Rally may exclude from time to time.

Rally reserves the right to determine whether a transaction qualifies as an Eligible Transaction.

Reversals, Refunds and Cancellations

If an Eligible Transaction is subsequently reversed, refunded, cancelled, or charged back, Rally may adjust the cumulative Pass utilisation associated with such transaction at its discretion.

If a transaction is reversed or refunded after the Pass has been exhausted, Rally may restore the corresponding Transaction Value to the user's Pass utilisation balance, subject to applicable system and processing conditions.

The purchase of a Rally Pass does not guarantee any minimum number or value of transactions.

Pass Fee — Refunds and Cancellation

The Pass Fee is payable upfront and, unless otherwise required under applicable law or expressly provided by Rally, is non-refundable once the Rally Pass has been activated or utilised.

A user may not cancel, transfer, exchange, or convert a Rally Pass or the Pass Fee into cash after activation.

If a Rally Pass has not been activated due to a technical or processing issue attributable to Rally, the Pass Fee may be refunded or otherwise resolved in accordance with Rally's applicable policies. The Pass Fee shall not be adjusted against the underlying Transaction Value.

Validity and Exhaustion

A Rally Pass shall remain valid until the earlier of:

  • the applicable Pass Limit being fully exhausted; or
  • any expiry date specifically communicated by Rally for the applicable Pass.

Where no expiry date is specified for a particular Rally Pass, the Pass shall remain available for use until its Pass Limit is exhausted, subject to these T&Cs and any applicable changes communicated by Rally.

Once a Rally Pass is exhausted, the user shall be charged applicable Platform Fees on subsequent transactions in accordance with the prevailing Platform Fees applicable on the Rally Platform. Rally shall not be obligated to automatically renew or extend an exhausted or expired Rally Pass.

The Pass Fee shall be subject to applicable taxes, including GST, as may be required under applicable law. Unless expressly stated otherwise, the Rally Pass does not cover statutory taxes, government levies, third-party charges, or other amounts that may become payable in connection with a transaction.

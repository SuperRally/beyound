Rally Referral — Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. About the Referral Program

The Rally Referral Program allows existing Rally users to invite friends to join Rally through a referral link. When a referred user completes a successful payment, both the referrer and the referred user can earn RallyCash based on the time taken to complete the payment.

2. Definitions

Referral: An invitation shared by an existing Rally user with a potential new user through a referral link.

Referrer: An existing Rally user who shares their referral link with another person.

Referred User: A new user who joins Rally through a valid referral link and completes a successful payment. Existing Rally users are not eligible to be referred users.

RallyCash: Reward credits that can be used to offset platform fees on the Rally app. RallyCash is not actual cash and cannot be withdrawn as money.

3. Referral Rewards

The reward depends on how quickly the referred user completes a successful payment after clicking the referral link.

Time to successful paymentReward (each user)
Within 3 minutes1,000 RallyCash
More than 3 minutes and up to 10 minutes500 RallyCash
More than 10 minutes and thereafter200 RallyCash

The timer starts when the referred user clicks the referral link. Only one reward tier applies to each successful referral.

4. Referral Eligibility

The referrer must share a valid referral link, and the referred user must use the link and complete a successful payment on Rally. Sharing a referral link alone does not qualify for rewards.

Each referral must relate to a genuine new user. Existing Rally users cannot qualify as referred users.

5. Reward Crediting

After the referred user's payment is successfully completed and verified, the applicable RallyCash will be credited to both users' Rally accounts.

Failed, cancelled, or reversed payments do not qualify for rewards.

6. Use of RallyCash

RallyCash can be used to offset eligible platform fees within the Rally app. It cannot be withdrawn or transferred to a bank account as cash. Its use is subject to the redemption options available within the app.

7. Referral Restrictions

Self-referrals, duplicate referrals, fake accounts, or attempts to misuse the referral program are not eligible for rewards. RallyCash credited through ineligible or fraudulent referrals may be withheld or reversed.

8. Changes to the Referral Program

Rally may update, pause, or discontinue the referral program from time to time. Any applicable changes will be communicated through the Rally app.

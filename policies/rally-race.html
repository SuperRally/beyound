1. Introduction

BeyoundExtra Tech Private Limited (“Beyound”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is conducting the Rally Race (“Campaign”) through the Rally application (“Rally App”).

The Campaign provides eligible users with an opportunity to participate in a leaderboard based on eligible bill payments, wallet payments/Rally Passes, and engagement with specified Rally features. Eligible users may stand a chance to win the rewards specified in these Terms and Conditions (“T&Cs”).

By participating in the Campaign, you agree to these T&Cs, the applicable terms governing the Rally App, and Beyound’s other applicable policies.

2. Definitions

For the purposes of these T&Cs:

2.1 “Campaign Period” means the period during which the Campaign is available, as communicated through the Rally App.

2.2 “Eligible User” means a user who satisfies the eligibility criteria specified under these T&Cs.

2.3 “Qualifying Transaction” means a transaction that satisfies the eligibility requirements of the Campaign and is successfully completed during the Campaign Period.

2.4 “Eligible Bill Payment Volume” means the aggregate value of eligible bill payment transactions considered for scoring during the Campaign Period.

2.5 “Wallet Payments/Rally Passes Value” means the eligible value of wallet payments/Rally Passes considered for scoring in accordance with the Campaign criteria.

2.6 “Eligible Volume” means the combined value of Eligible Bill Payment Volume and Wallet Payments/Rally Passes value, calculated in accordance with these T&Cs.

2.7 “Base Points” means the points calculated by dividing the Eligible Volume by ₹1,000.

2.8 “Engagement Multiplier” means the additional percentage applied to Base Points for each eligible referral or qualifying Rally feature activity specified in these T&Cs.

2.9 “Final Score” means the score calculated by applying the total applicable Engagement Multipliers to the Base Points.

2.10 “Leaderboard” means the ranking of Eligible Users maintained and displayed through the Rally App based on their Final Scores.

2.11 “Reward” means the bike awarded to each Winner under the Campaign.

2.12 “Winner” means an Eligible User who secures one of the three highest positions on the final verified Leaderboard, subject to successful verification and compliance with these T&Cs.

2.13 “DFA” means Debt Free Age. DFA is a feature on the Rally App that provides the user with an indicative estimate of the age at which the user may become free of outstanding debt, based on the information provided by or available in respect of the user. The DFA estimate is illustrative only and does not constitute financial advice or a guarantee of any outcome.

2.14 “KYC” means the Know Your Customer verification process applicable to a user on the Rally App, including the submission, validation and verification of the user’s identity details and documents, as may be required by Beyound in accordance with applicable law and the Rally App’s verification requirements.

5. Eligible Volume and Base Points

5.1 Calculation of Eligible Volume

Eligible Volume shall be calculated as follows:

Eligible Volume = Eligible Bill Payment Volume + Wallet Payments/Rally Passes Value

5.2 Eligible bill payment volume shall be considered at 100% of its eligible transaction value.

5.3 Wallet payments/Rally Passes shall carry equal weight for the applicable scoring component.

5.4 The same transaction or value shall not be counted more than once.

5.5 Calculation of Base Points

One Base Point shall be awarded for every ₹1,000 of Eligible Volume.

Base Points = Eligible Volume ÷ ₹1,000

5.6 Illustrative Example

If a user has an Eligible Bill Payment Volume of ₹40,000 and a wallet payments/Rally Passes value of ₹2,00,000:

  • Eligible Volume = ₹40,000 + ₹2,00,000 = ₹2,40,000.
  • Base Points = ₹2,40,000 ÷ ₹1,000 = 240 points.

This example is illustrative and subject to the Campaign’s eligibility criteria.

6.5 The total applicable Engagement Multiplier shall be the product of the multiplier factors for all qualifying activities completed by the user during the Campaign Period.

Merely initiating or submitting a KYC request shall not qualify for the multiplier unless the verification is successfully completed.

7. Final Score and Leaderboard Ranking

7.1 The Final Score of each Eligible User shall be calculated by applying the total applicable Engagement Multipliers to the Base Points.

Final Score = Base Points × ∏(1 + Freq × Engagement Multiplier)

Where “Freq” means the number of times the user has completed a qualifying activity during the Campaign Period, “Engagement Multiplier” means the multiplier applicable to that activity under Clause 6.2, and “∏” means the product of the multiplier factors across all qualifying activities. The Engagement Multipliers shall be expressed as percentages in the calculation.

7.2 Illustrative Example

Assume a user has:

  • Eligible Bill Payment Volume: ₹40,000;
  • Wallet payments/Rally Passes value: ₹10,00,000;
  • DFA Initialization: 1 time (20%);
  • Class Upgrade Initialization: 1 time (20%); and
  • Qualifying referrals: 4 (10% each).

The calculation shall be:

  1. Eligible Volume = ₹40,000 + ₹10,00,000 = ₹10,40,000.
  2. Base Points = ₹10,40,000 ÷ ₹1,000 = 1,040 points.
  3. Applicable Engagement Multipliers:
    • DFA Initialization: 1 + (1 × 20%) = 1.20;
    • Class Upgrade Initialization: 1 + (1 × 20%) = 1.20;
    • Qualifying referrals: 1 + (4 × 10%) = 1.40.
  4. Final Score = Base Points × ∏(1 + Freq × Engagement Multiplier) = 1,040 × (1.20 × 1.20 × 1.40) = 1,040 × 2.016 = 2,096.64 points.

This example is illustrative and is subject to verification of the user’s transactions and activities.
